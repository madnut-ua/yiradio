How many percent of volume one click of the encoder or one press of a remote volume key moves.

Changing station

On a change of station or source the sound rises from silence to the set volume over two seconds. A change of track in files or in a wave is as before.

FM radio

0

FM's loudness against internet radio and files. Raise it if FM is noticeably quieter; at a high setting the loudest moments may be clipped.

Sound output

The sound goes to the digital S/PDIF output instead of the built-in DAC. Recordings above 48 kHz go out at half or a quarter of their rate. Bluetooth from a phone comes here too when the module's DIN wire goes to the ESP32, otherwise it plays through the DAC. While a Bluetooth speaker is connected the sound is its.

          Ready

          The archive holds everything the device knows about itself: Wi-Fi networks, settings, the Ya.Music token, the weather key, the keys the remote was taught and the FM stations with their pictures. The playlist is saved separately, on the Stations page.

          Download the archive

          Restore the whole archive or a single file out of it — wifi.json, settings.csv, yandex.json, weather.json, remote.csv, fm_presets.csv or levels.csv. The device restarts afterwards.

          The Wi-Fi passwords, the Ya.Music token and the weather key are in the archive as plain text: keep it the way you would keep a password.
